A neutral dark theme. Graphite instead of Discord's blue-tinted greys, and a little darker than Darker.

## Palette

- Chat: `#121212`
- Channel list and DMs: `#181818`
- Server rail: `#0c0c0a`
- Cards, embeds and the composer: `#1c1c1c`
- Text: `#eeeeef`, muted text `#929394`
- Accent on buttons, switches and reactions: `#7d7569`
- Links: `#c9b39a`, mentions: `#b08a52`

## Details

- Built on the `darker` base type, so every screen Discord has a token for follows the palette.
- Warm iOS system colours are included, which removes the blue tint from native elements too.
- Discord's native swipe-to-reply indicator is drawn natively and cannot be recoloured by a theme. The [Nocturne Native](https://unbound.rip/addons/1esthetic.nocturne-native) plugin does that part.
- iPhone, iPad and Android.
